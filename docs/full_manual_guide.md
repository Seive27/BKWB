## How to use this guide

This manual is written in plain language. You do not need technical experience to follow it.

1. Find your role in **Who uses BKWBS**.
2. Complete the **Onboarding** steps for your role.
3. Use the **Walkthroughs** when you need a step-by-step reminder.
4. Use **Training & Support** when you need help, refresher training, or self-service answers.

---

## 1. What is BKWBS?

BKWBS is the official water billing and service information system of **Barangay Kalunasan**.

It helps the barangay:

- manage resident water accounts;
- record meter readings;
- generate and track bills;
- record payments and receipts;
- send announcements;
- handle service concerns through tickets;
- keep secure audit records of important system activity.

## 2. Who uses BKWBS?

| Role | App / access | Main job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Super Admin** | Desktop / web Super Admin dashboard | Manage users, settings, analytics, and audit logs |
| **Barangay Staff** | Desktop / web Staff dashboard | Manage residents, readings, billing, payments, announcements, and tickets |
| **Meter Reader** | Meter Reader mobile app | Capture and upload assigned meter readings with dial photos |
| **Resident** | Resident mobile app | View bills, pay, read announcements, update profile, and file tickets |

## 3. Getting started (all users)

### 3.1 Before you log in

You need:

- an official BKWBS account created by Barangay Staff or Super Admin;
- your registered email address;
- a strong password (or a temporary password given during onboarding);
- the correct app for your role (desktop/web for staff roles; mobile for residents and meter readers).

### 3.2 First login checklist

1. Open the correct app for your role.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Change your temporary password if you were given one.
4. Confirm your profile details (name, contact number, address when applicable).
5. Read the [Acceptable Use Policy](./ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md).
6. Complete the short role walkthrough in Section 5.

### 3.3 Forgot password

1. On the login screen, choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email linked to your account.
3. Enter the one-time code (OTP) sent to your email.
4. Create a new password and sign in again.

If you do not receive an OTP within a few minutes, check spam/junk, confirm the email spelling, then contact Barangay Staff for account verification.

## 4. Onboarding process for new users

### 4.1 Standard onboarding path

| Step | What happens | Who does it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Account request / enrollment | Resident applies for service, or staff/meter reader is assigned system access | Barangay Hall / Super Admin |
| 2. Account creation | Official BKWBS account is created with the correct role | Super Admin or authorized Staff |
| 3. Credentials handoff | Email and temporary password (or reset link instructions) are given securely | Super Admin / Staff |
| 4. Orientation | Short walkthrough of the app and security rules | Trainer / Staff lead |
| 5. Guided practice | New user completes 1–2 real or practice tasks | New user + trainer |
| 6. Acknowledgement | User confirms understanding of AUP and role duties | New user |
| 7. Go-live access | Full operational use begins | Approved |

### 4.2 Training materials provided at onboarding

New users receive:

- this **Full Manual Guide**;
- a **role walkthrough** (Section 5);
- the **Acceptable Use Policy**;
- a short **security briefing** (passwords, privacy, no account sharing);
- where applicable, a hands-on demo on the staff desktop or mobile app.

### 4.3 Role-specific onboarding focus

**Super Admin**

- Create/edit users and roles
- Review analytics and audit logs
- Schedule/communicate maintenance windows
- Confirm system settings before staff go-live

**Barangay Staff**

- Register or update residents
- Review meter readings
- Generate bills and post payments
- Create announcements
- Manage service tickets end-to-end

**Meter Reader**

- Open assigned reading routes/accounts
- Capture accurate dial readings
- Take clear meter dial photos
- Flag meter anomalies
- Sync/upload readings before leaving the field when possible

**Resident**

- Log in to the Resident app
- View current bill and payment status
- Read announcements and water schedule
- Update personal contact information
- Create a support ticket when there is a service concern

## 5. Walkthroughs by role

### 5.1 Super Admin walkthrough

1. Sign in to the Super Admin dashboard.
2. Open **Users** to confirm staff, meter readers, and other accounts.
3. Open **Analytics** to review system activity at a high level.
4. Open **Audit Logs** when investigating access or data changes.
5. Review **Settings** before announcing rate or system changes.
6. Sign out when finished, especially on shared barangay computers.

### 5.2 Barangay Staff walkthrough

1. Sign in to the Staff dashboard.
2. Check **Dashboard** for today’s overview (residents, bills, pending items).
3. Open **Residents** to search, view, or update a resident account.
4. Open **Meter Readings** to review submitted readings and statuses.
5. Open **Bills** to generate or monitor billing for the period.
6. Open **Payments** to record/verify payments and issue receipts.
7. Open **Announcements** to publish barangay notices.
8. Open **Tickets / Messages** to assign, update, and resolve concerns.
9. Open **Reports** when summaries are needed for operations or leadership.
10. Use **Profile Settings** to update your own account details, then sign out.

### 5.3 Meter Reader walkthrough

1. Open the Meter Reader mobile app and sign in.
2. Review today’s assigned accounts/readings.
3. At each meter: enter the reading carefully.
4. Take a clear photo of the dial.
5. Log any visible damage, stuck dial, or suspected tampering.
6. Submit/upload the reading.
7. Continue until the assigned list is complete.
8. Confirm all submissions succeeded before ending the shift.

### 5.4 Resident walkthrough

1. Open the Resident mobile app and sign in.
2. Check the home/dashboard for bill status and alerts.
3. Open **Bills / Payments** to view amount due and payment history.
4. Open **Announcements** for interruptions, billing reminders, and advisories.
5. Open **Tickets** if you have a concern (water supply, billing, plumbing, water quality, meter, or other).
6. Update **Profile** if your phone number or personal details change.
7. Sign out on shared devices.

## 7. Service tickets (support inside the system)

Residents can file tickets for concerns such as:

- water supply
- billing
- plumbing
- water quality
- meter concern
- other

### Ticket statuses you may see

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Newly created; waiting for staff action |
| Assigned | A staff member is responsible |
| In Progress | Work has started |
| Resolved | Fix or answer has been provided |
| Closed | Ticket is complete |

Tips for faster resolution:

- choose the correct category;
- write a clear subject and description;
- include account name/address and useful photos when available;
- check ticket updates in the app before visiting the hall for the same issue.

### 8.2 Onboarding process summary

New users are onboarded through:

1. account creation with the correct role;
2. secure credential handoff;
3. orientation using this Full Manual Guide and role walkthroughs;
4. guided practice of core tasks;
5. acknowledgement of the Acceptable Use Policy and security rules;
6. supervised first-week support as needed.

### 8.3 Ongoing training and refreshers

| Training | Who | When | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New-user orientation | All new users | Before first official use | Learn the app and basic security |
| Role refresher | Staff / Meter Readers / Super Admins | At least once a year, or after major feature updates | Keep skills current |
| Annual security training | All users with system access | Every 12 months | Passwords, privacy, phishing, acceptable use |
| Process update briefing | Affected roles | When billing, payment, reading, or ticket workflows change | Prevent errors after system changes |
| Just-in-time coaching | Any user needing help | As requested through support channels | Fix specific task blockers quickly |

Annual security training covers at minimum:

- protecting login credentials;
- never sharing accounts;
- handling resident personal data carefully;
- recognizing suspicious emails or fake reset requests;
- reporting lost devices or possible unauthorized access immediately.

### 8.5 Response time expectations

These are the standard targets for BKWBS support requests. Urgent public-safety or major outage issues may be handled sooner.

| Request type | Examples | Target first response | Target resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Critical** | Cannot bill/pay system-wide; widespread login outage; suspected data breach | Within **4 office hours** | Within **1–2 office days**, with temporary workaround if needed |
| **High** | Resident cannot access own account; payment/receipt error affecting due date; meter reading upload failure blocking daily route | Within **1 office day** | Within **3 office days** |
| **Normal** | How-to questions; profile updates; non-urgent ticket follow-up; report clarification | Within **2 office days** | Within **5 office days** |
| **Low / informational** | General FAQ, feature suggestions, non-blocking documentation questions | Within **3 office days** | As scheduled |

Office-hour note: response clocks generally run during Barangay Hall operating days/hours, unless an emergency requires after-hours action.

#### Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**  
A: Use **Forgot password** on the login screen, enter your email, then use the OTP code to set a new password.

**Q: I did not receive an OTP.**  
A: Wait a few minutes, check spam/junk, confirm the email, then ask Barangay Staff to verify your account email.

**Q: Can I share my account with a family member or co-worker?**  
A: No. Each person must use their own account. Sharing accounts violates the Acceptable Use Policy.

**Q: Where do I report no water / dirty water / a broken meter?**  
A: Residents should create a ticket in the Resident app and choose the closest category. For emergencies during office hours, also contact the Barangay Hall.

**Q: My bill looks wrong.**  
A: Check the latest meter reading and payment history in the app, then create a **billing** ticket or visit the Help Desk with your account details.

**Q: The Meter Reader app will not upload.**  
A: Check mobile signal/Wi-Fi, try again, and do not delete the reading draft if still available. Escalate to Staff/Super Admin if upload keeps failing.

**Q: Who can change water rates?**  
A: Only authorized Staff/Super Admin through approved system workflows.

**Q: Where is the official Full Manual Guide link for compliance submissions?**  
A: https://github.com/Seive27/BKWB/blob/real-db/docs/full_manual_guide.md

## 9. Security reminders for every user

- Keep your password private.
- Lock or sign out of shared devices.
- Do not take or send screenshots of other people’s personal data unless required for official work.
- Report lost phones or suspected unauthorized access immediately to Staff or Super Admin.
- Follow the [Acceptable Use Policy](./ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md).
